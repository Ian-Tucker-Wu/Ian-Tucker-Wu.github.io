abrasive n

a substance for grinding, smoothing, or polishing (used an abrasive for polishing the rough stones)

abrasive adj

1 causing damage or wear by rubbing (abrasive compounds for whitening teeth) 2 very unpleasant or irritating (abrasive compounds for whitening teeth)

abreast adv or adj

1 right beside one another (the street was only wide enough for four people to walk abreast) 2 up to a certain level of knowledge (I try to keep abreast of the news.)

abridge vb

to shorten by leaving out some parts (abridge a dictionary)

abridgement n

a shortned form of a written work (an abridgement of a biography)

abroad adv or adj

1 over a wide area (The tree branches spread abroad) 2 outdoors (Few people are abroad at 00:00) 3 in or to a foreign country (travel abroad) 4 known to many people (The news is abroad)

abrupt adj

1 sudden (The meeting came to an abrupt end) 2 steep (an abrupt drop) 3 rudely brief (an abrupt reply)

abscess n

a collection of pus with swollen and red tissue around it (After eating for weeks with a severe toothache, he finally went to the dentist and discovered it had developed into an abscess.)

absence n

1 not there at a usual or expected place (an absence at school) 2 lack, want (there was an absence of affection amoung the people.)

absent adj

1 not here (I missed a trip when I was absent) 2 nonexisting (Trees are absent in the desert) 3 showing lack of attention (an absent stare)

absentee n

a person who is absent (this donkey was an absentee)

absentminded adj

tending to forget or not pay attention (3 absentminded people forgot to get their wallet at the cafe)

absoulute adj

1 total, complete (absoulute darkness) 2 2 not limited in any way (absoulute power) 3 free from doubt (absoulute proof)

absolve vb

to be free from guilt or responsibility (He was absolved from drinking.)

absorb vb

1 to take in or swallow up (absorbing water) 2 to hold the complete attention of (he was absorbed by the movie) 3 to receibe without giving back (the walls of the room absorb sound)
