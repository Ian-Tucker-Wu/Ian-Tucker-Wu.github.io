absence n

1 not there at a usual or expected place (an absence at school) 2 lack, want (there was an absence of affection amoung the people.)

absent adj

1 not here (I missed a trip when I was absent) 2 nonexisting (Trees are absent in the desert) 3 showing lack of attention (an absent stare)

absentee n

a person who is absent (this donkey was an absentee)

absentminded adj

tending to forget or not pay attention (3 absentminded people forgot to get their wallet at the cafe)

absoulute adj

1 total, complete (absoulute darkness) 2 2 not limited in any way (absoulute power) 3 free from doubt (absoulute proof)

absolve vb

to be free from guilt or responsibility (He was absolved from drinking.)

absorb vb

1 to take in or swallow up (absorbing water) 2 to hold the complete attention of (he was absorbed by the movie) 3 to receibe without giving back (the walls of the room absorb sound)
